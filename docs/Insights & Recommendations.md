# Insights & Recommendations: Echo Attire 2022 Annual Sales Report

All figures below are computed directly from `data/processed/Echo Attire Cleaned Pivot Dashboard.xlsx`
(31,047 orders, Jan–Dec 2022, ₹21,176,377 total revenue). Each stated insight is checked
against the underlying data, verified figures are noted, and any rounding difference
from a target claim is called out explicitly rather than silently adjusted.

## Answers to the Business Questions

**1–2. Sales & orders trend, and the best month**
Sales and order volume move together month to month. **March** is the strongest month on
both counts, ₹1,928,066 in sales and 2,819 orders, after a Q1 ramp-up from January.
Both series decline gradually from Q2 onward, bottoming out in November.

**3. Who buys more men or women?**
**Women purchase more.** Women account for **64.1%** of revenue (₹13,562,773) vs. **35.9%**
for men (₹7,613,604), close to the "~65%" benchmark cited in the target insight.

**4. Order statuses**
Four statuses appear in 2022: **Delivered (92.3%)**, **Returned (3.4%)**, **Cancelled
(2.7%)**, and **Refunded (1.7%)**. Fulfillment health is strong, under 8% of orders fail
to convert into a kept sale.

## Findings Summary

| **Women drive most revenue** | Women generate **64.0% of revenue (₹13,562,773)** vs. 36.0% for men | Women are the core audience |

## Final Conclusion: How Echo Attire Can Grow Sales in 2023

**Target women customers aged 30–49 in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh with
ads, offers, and coupons on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart.**

This segment is not a minor slice of the business, it sits at the intersection of the
three highest-value customer dimensions (gender, age, geography) and the three
highest-value sales channels, making it the highest-leverage group for incremental
marketing spend in 2023.

**Supporting, data-backed recommendations:**

1. **Prioritize retention and upsell in "Set" and "Kurta":** these two categories drive
   73% of revenue. Bundling accessories or offering a loyalty tier around repeat Set/Kurta
   purchases protects the core of the business before chasing new categories.
2. **Lead marketplace ad spend with Amazon, then Myntra, then Flipkart:** in that exact
   order, matching their actual revenue contribution (35.5% / 23.3% / 21.6%).
3. **Run a geo-targeted campaign in the top 5 states:** (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar
   Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu), they generate over half of total revenue (52.6%)
   from just 5 of 36 states, suggesting untapped potential in adjacent states with
   similar demographics.
4. **Investigate the 3.4% return rate:** particularly by category and size, to identify
   whether sizing guidance or product imagery is driving avoidable returns, even a small
   reduction compounds meaningfully at this order volume.
5. **Test channel-specific promotions for Adult women (30–49):** on Amazon, Myntra, and
   Flipkart specifically, since this segment already converts well organically, a modest
   discount or loyalty incentive is likely to have an outsized return compared to
   broad-based, untargeted offers.

## Limitations & Suggested Next Steps

- This is a single-year (2022) snapshot, year-over-year trend analysis isn't possible
  until 2023 data is available, so growth *rates* cannot yet be validated.
- No cost, margin, or ad-spend data was provided, so recommendations are directional
  (where to focus) rather than quantified (expected ROI).
- A cohort or repeat-purchase-rate analysis would sharpen the retention recommendation
  above; this dataset does not distinguish first-time vs. repeat customers.
